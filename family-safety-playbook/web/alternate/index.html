A peek inside

One of the 10 Quick-Reference Cards

CARD 2

"Am I being followed?" — the 3-turn test

  1. Make three ordinary turns you'd have no normal reason to take.
  2. Still with you after all three? Treat it as a follow. Don't speed, panic or confront.
  3. Drive to safety, never home — police post, filling station, bank, or a busy, well-lit place.
  4. Call someone and say it out loud: where you are, where you're heading, the car's colour and plate.

The book is full of these: scripts to say out loud, checklists, and a worksheet at the end of every module, so you finish with your family's own written plan.
