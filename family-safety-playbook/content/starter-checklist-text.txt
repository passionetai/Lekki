12 things every Nigerian family should do this week

 Who it's for: every Nigerian family — whether you live in a gated estate or a face-me-I-face-you

💪
 The families who stay safe aren't lucky — they're prepared. This isn't about fear or

Just a plan that works.

TRAVEL
MONEY & ONLINE
Agree on a family code word that means "something is wrong — do exactly what I say, now."☐
Vary your routes and times — avoid being predictable, especially to and from home.☐
At ATMs, stay alert, keep your phone away, and scan around before you step away.☐
Lock gates and doors every single time — and never let children open the gate for anyone
without an adult.
☐
Agree a safe room and a family meeting point if something happens.☐
Confirm your child's school pickup password and authorised-collector list.☐
Make sure each child knows your phone number and one trusted adult's number by heart.☐
On long or late trips, share your live location with one trusted person — and turn it off when you
arrive.
☐
Never click "verify your account" links from SMS or WhatsApp. When in doubt, call your bank's
official line.
☐
Turn on transaction alerts and set a sensible daily transfer limit.☐
