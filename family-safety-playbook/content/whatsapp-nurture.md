## Weekly tips (starters; soft pitch every 4–5)
- **ATM:** Finish, put your phone away, then scan around before you step away. Most follow-attacks start with a distracted exit.
- **Kids & the gate:** Teach children never to open the gate for anyone — even someone in uniform — without an adult. Practise it once this week.
- **Travel:** On long or late trips, share your live location with one trusted person. Turn it off when you arrive.
- **Money:** Never tap "verify your account" links from SMS or WhatsApp. Banks don't ask that way — when in doubt, call the official line.
